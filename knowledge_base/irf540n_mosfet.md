# IRF540N Power MOSFET — Technical Datasheet Summary

- **Part Number**: IRF540N (N-Channel Enhancement-Mode Silicon Power MOSFET)
- **Package**: TO-220AB
- **Absolute Maximum Drain-Source Voltage (Vds)**: 100 V
- **Gate-Source Voltage (Vgs Max)**: +-20 V
- **Gate Threshold Voltage (Vgs(th))**: 2.0 V min to 4.0 V max
- **Continuous Drain Current (Id at 25 C)**: 33 A (23 A at 100 C)
- **Static Drain-Source On-Resistance (Rds(on) max)**: 44 mOhm (0.044 Ohm) at Vgs = 10 V, Id = 16 A
- **Operating Junction Temperature (Tj)**: -55 C to +175 C
- **Gate Charge (Qg max)**: 71 nC
- **Typical Applications**: High-frequency switching regulators, DC motor PWM drivers, synchronous rectifiers, BMS cutoff switches.
- **Thermal Derating Note**: Exceeding Vds = 100 V or operating above Tj = 175 C causes avalanche breakdown unless clamped with a flyback diode on inductive loads.
